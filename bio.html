Professional summary

A hands-on technical leader with operator discipline.

Walter Rodriguez has more than two decades of experience managing client relationships, leading local and remote technical teams, and delivering VoIP, data, networking, healthcare communications, AI-enabled support, and cloud services. His background spans support leadership, service delivery management, network operations, sales engineering, field engineering, data center operations, carrier services, and cloud migration planning.

As Director of Support at SpinSci Technologies, Walter leads global support operations for AI-powered healthcare communication platforms, helping mission-critical environments improve patient access, reliability, performance, and customer experience at scale.

At Quantum Cloud Solutions, Walter brings that enterprise-grade experience into practical support for homes and small businesses: computer repair, secure networking, router setup, Microsoft cloud services, remote troubleshooting, VoIP configuration, privacy protection, and cyber-forensics support.
